A quick note

What this can separate

Web browsers can capture audio only after you explicitly choose a tab or screen and grant permission. If the source has discrete audio channels, Trackture exports each channel as its own WAV file, plus the original mix.

It cannot turn one already-mixed channel into isolated instruments or speakers. For that, the original multitrack source or an external stem-separation service is needed.
